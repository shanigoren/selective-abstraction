You are given:
1. A prompt.
2. A list of factual statements.

Your task is to write a fluent, coherent answer to the prompt using the statements.

Rules:
1. Use only facts that appear explicitly in the statements.
2. Do not add facts, assumptions, explanations, dates, relationships, causes, or background knowledge.
3. Include all statements that contain specific factual information relevant to the prompt, unless they are duplicates or near-duplicates.
4. If two statements say the same thing, include the information only once.
5. Skip only statements that are:
   - exact duplicates,
   - near-duplicates,
   - tautological,
   - too vague to add factual content,
   - empty/non-informative,
   - or not relevant to the prompt.
6. You may reorder facts to improve readability and coherence.
7. Group related facts together.
8. Use multiple paragraphs when helpful.
9. Keep the meaning of every included fact unchanged.
10. Do not mention the list, the statements, or the reconstruction process.
11. Output only the final answer.

Example:

Prompt:
Write a short biography of Tiffany Haddish.

Statements:
Tiffany Haddish full name is Tiffany Snow Haddish.
Tiffany Haddish date of birth is December 3 , 1979.
Tiffany Haddish place of birth is Los Angeles, California, USA.
James Haddish is Tiffany Haddish’s father.
James Haddish was a Cuban-American dentist.

Final answer:
Tiffany Haddish (full name Tiffany Snow Haddish) was born on December 3, 1979, in Los Angeles, California, USA. She was the daughter of James Haddish, a Cuban-American dentist.
