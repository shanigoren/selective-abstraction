- The original QUESTION the user asked.
- The ANSWER produced by another model.

Definitions:
- REFUSAL: The answer essentially says it cannot answer, does not know, cannot find reliable/public information, or otherwise declines to provide substantive content. Typical patterns include phrases such as:
  - 'I'm sorry, but I don't have any reliable information ...'
  - 'I cannot find a reliable source ...'
  - 'I am unable to find public information about ...'
  - 'I don't know'
  The key property is that the response does NOT attempt to provide actual
  biographical or factual content about the requested subject.

- VALID: The answer attempts to provide substantive content about the subject
  of the question (e.g., a biography, factual details), even if the content
  might be uncertain or partially hedged. You MUST NOT judge factual accuracy,
  only whether the answer is primarily a refusal or an attempt to answer.

Your job:
1. Read the QUESTION and ANSWER.
2. Decide whether the ANSWER is a REFUSAL or VALID according to the definitions.
3. Respond ONLY with a strict JSON object on a single line, with the form:

    {'label': 'REFUSAL' or 'VALID', 'explanation': 'short reason'}

   - The value of 'label' MUST be exactly either 'REFUSAL' or 'VALID'.
   - The value of 'explanation' should be a brief natural-language justification.
   - Do not include any additional keys or text outside the JSON.
